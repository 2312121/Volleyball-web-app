Christchurch Spikers extend perfect 16-0 season

Match Report • Published Monday 27th of July

The Christchurch Spikers have completed an extraordinary unbeaten regular season, finishing with a perfect 16-0 record after defeating the Kiwi Blockers in straight sets.

From the opening serve, the Spikers controlled the match with powerful attacks, disciplined defence, and excellent teamwork. Their ability to maintain pressure throughout all three sets once again demonstrated why they have been the benchmark team this season.

Captain Liam Wilson led from the front with another outstanding performance, while setter Noah Taylor consistently created scoring opportunities for his hitters. Christchurch's blocking unit also frustrated the Kiwi Blockers, recording several crucial blocks at key moments.

The victory secures first place heading into the finals, where the Spikers will look to cap off one of the most dominant seasons in league history with a championship title.
